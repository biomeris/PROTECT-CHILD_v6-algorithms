References
==========

- Rencher, A. C., & Christensen, W. F. (2012). *Methods of Multivariate
  Analysis* (3rd ed.), chapter 6. Wiley. (One-way MANOVA, the four test
  statistics.)
- Rao, C. R. (1951). An asymptotic expansion of the distribution of Wilks'
  criterion. *Bulletin of the International Statistical Institute*, 33(2),
  177-180. (F approximation for Wilks' lambda.)
- SAS Institute. *SAS/STAT User's Guide*, "Multivariate Tests" in the
  introduction to regression procedures. (F approximations for all four
  statistics, as also used by ``statsmodels``.)
- Seabold, S., & Perktold, J. (2010). statsmodels: Econometric and statistical
  modeling with Python. *Proceedings of the 9th Python in Science
  Conference*. (Reference implementation used for validation.)
