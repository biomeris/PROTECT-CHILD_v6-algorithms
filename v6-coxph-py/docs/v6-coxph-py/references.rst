References
==========

- Cox, D. R. (1972). Regression Models and Life-Tables. *Journal of the Royal
  Statistical Society: Series B (Methodological)*, 34(2), 187-202. (Defines
  the partial likelihood underlying this implementation.)
- Andersen, P. K., & Gill, R. D. (1982). Cox's Regression Model for Counting
  Processes: A Large Sample Study. *The Annals of Statistics*, 10(4),
  1100-1120. (Basis for the counting-process / Wald-test justification used
  for the overall model significance test.)
- Akaike, H. (1974). A new look at the statistical model identification.
  *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control*, 19(6), 716-723. (AIC formula.)
- Lu, C.-L., Wang, S., Ji, Z., Wu, Y., Xiong, L., Jiang, X., & Ohno-Machado, L.
  (2015). WebDISCO: a web service for distributed cox model learning without
  patient-level data sharing. *Journal of the American Medical Informatics
  Association*, 22(6), 1212-1219. (The distributed risk-set-aggregate
  approach this federated decomposition follows.)
