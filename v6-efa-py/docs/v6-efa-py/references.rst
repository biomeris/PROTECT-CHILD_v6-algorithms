References
==========

- Revelle, W. ``psych``: Procedures for Psychological, Psychometric, and
  Personality Research. R package, function ``fa``. (Minres and ML extraction,
  starting values, sign and order conventions.)
- Biggs, J., & Madnani, N. ``factor_analyzer``: Python package, version 0.5.1.
  (Reference implementation used for validation.)
- Kaiser, H. F. (1958). The varimax criterion for analytic rotation in factor
  analysis. *Psychometrika*, 23(3), 187-200.
- Hendrickson, A. E., & White, P. O. (1964). Promax: A quick method for
  rotation to oblique simple structure. *British Journal of Statistical
  Psychology*, 17(1), 65-70.
- Jennrich, R. I. (2002). A simple general method for oblique rotation.
  *Psychometrika*, 67(1), 7-19. (Gradient projection, used for oblimin.)
- Kaiser, H. F. (1974). An index of factorial simplicity. *Psychometrika*,
  39(1), 31-36. (KMO.)
- Bartlett, M. S. (1950). Tests of significance in factor analysis. *British
  Journal of Statistical Psychology*, 3(2), 77-85.
